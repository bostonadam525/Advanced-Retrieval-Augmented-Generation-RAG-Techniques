Question: Why did the British come to India?

Answer:

Final Answer: The British came to India primarily to exploit its wealth and resources—collecting taxes, using Indian labor and raw materials, and selling British goods—but also to secure a strategic foothold that supplied a huge proportion of the empire’s troops and gave Britain global military and political leverage.
